How to play
  • 🎭 Everyone gets a secret role. The Mafia know each other.
  • 🌙 Night: cameras and mics go dark. The Mafia pick a victim, the Nurse protects someone, the Ranger investigates someone.
  • 🌅 Dawn: find out who was attacked.
  • ☀️ Day: talk on video, then tap a tile to vote. Most votes is sent home. A tie sends nobody.
  • 🏁 The Campers win when all the Mafia are gone. The Mafia win when they are as many as everyone else.

🔪 The Mafia: Pick a victim each night. Act innocent by day.

🩹 Camp Nurse: Protect one camper each night.

🔦 The Ranger: Investigate one camper each night.

🏕️ Camper: Find the Mafia and vote them out.

🤡 The Prankster: Get yourself voted out by day.
