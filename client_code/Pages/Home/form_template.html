Someone at camp is not who they seem.

A video party game for 3 to 10 friends. Everyone gets a secret role. At night the Mafia strike while the camp sleeps. By day you look each other in the eye, argue it out on video, and vote someone onto the bus home.

No account needed. Your browser will ask for your camera and microphone.

How a game goes

  1. 🔥

    Gather

    Start a camp and share its 4-letter code. Up to 10 people join a tiny-tile video call, straight from browser to browser.

  2. 🌙

    Night

    Cameras and mics go dark. Only the Mafia see and hear each other while they pick a victim. The Nurse and the Ranger work in secret.

  3. ☀️

    Day

    Wake up to the news. Talk, accuse, defend. Then everyone taps a tile to vote. Most votes is sent home.

Good to know

  • Headphones stop echo when ten people talk at once.
  • 6 to 10 players is the sweet spot. 3 works for a quick try.
  • Laptop or phone. Each person sends a small video to everyone, about 1.5 Mbps up for a full camp.
  • Ghosts (players who are out) keep watching, and can whisper with other ghosts.
